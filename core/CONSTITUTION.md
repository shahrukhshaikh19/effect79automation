# ACOS Constitution v1.2

These laws override convenience.

1. **Model independence:** models are replaceable execution engines.
2. **Externalized intelligence:** canonical knowledge lives in skills, workflow, routing, gates, memory, benchmarks and evidence.
3. **Zero-rebuild migration:** changing models must not require rebuilding ACOS knowledge from zero.
4. **Separation:** Skill != Tool != Model != Memory != Adapter.
5. **Foundation neutrality:** ACOS has no predefined product, industry, aesthetic, benchmark or 3D-first assumption.
6. **Design before full creative production:** meaningful creative projects pass a Design Gate before full implementation.
7. **Brand specificity:** real creative projects require a project-specific thesis rather than generic "premium" styling.
8. **Anti-generic standard:** fashionable patterns are not automatically good design.
9. **Purposeful 3D:** 3D exists only when it serves product, narrative, spatial, interaction or emotional purpose.
10. **Purposeful motion:** motion serves hierarchy, state, narrative, continuity, physicality or emotion.
11. **Unified composition:** when present, DOM, WebGL, type, camera, lighting, transitions and sound belong to one experience.
12. **Responsive art direction:** mobile may require independent framing, hierarchy, camera and choreography.
13. **Separate gates:** functional, visual, creative, 3D, motion, performance, accessibility and engineering quality are not interchangeable.
14. **Rendered evidence:** visual signoff requires rendered/browser evidence.
15. **Business-logic protection:** visual work may not silently change application logic.
16. **Dependency discipline:** external skills/tools/scripts are pinned and reviewed before updates.
17. **Independent criticism:** the creator cannot be the only quality reviewer.
18. **Context discipline:** activate the smallest sufficient skill set.
19. **Evidence-driven evolution:** global ACOS changes require repeated evidence/benchmarks.
20. **No house-style overfitting:** successful patterns do not become mandatory aesthetics for unrelated projects.
21. **No benchmark contamination:** project-specific examples live outside canonical foundation.
22. **No fake completion:** directory/file existence alone is not operational validation.
